Research interests & PhD aims

Interests

  • Dense 3D reconstruction from sequential 360° street-view imagery, with architectural judgment on form and occlusion
  • Novel pedestrian-path views from reconstructed scenes — filling SVI viewpoint gaps for path-level analysis
  • Urban risk and street-space analysis from street-level photography when a full 3D model is not the starting point

PhD aims

I am preparing PhD applications to deepen geometry- and vision-based methods for measuring cities from everyday imagery — connecting reconstruction, semantics, and application-level urban questions. I look for groups where spatial computing, photogrammetry / 3D vision, and urban analytics meet, and where an architecture background is an asset rather than a side track.
